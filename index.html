[ ABOUT GURMEHAR ]

CREATIVE DESIGNER & VIDEO EDITOR

I am Gurmehar Singh. I work with brands and companies to shoot, edit, and shape their social media presence. Whether producing high-impact vertical reels for mobile feeds or widescreen horizontal films, I focus on clean aesthetics, rhythm, and storytelling that commands attention.

I handle the full creative pipeline: concept moodboarding, on-location cinematography, video editing in Premiere Pro, kinetic typography in After Effects, and sound design. Having worked with brands like Mercedes, Amazon Fashion India, Infollion, CZI Global, Healthy Nutrition, and WanderOn, I know how to make your brand look modern, professional, and visually unforgettable.
